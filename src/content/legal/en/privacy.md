## Summary

This site is a static public page. It does not create accounts, does not ask you to upload documents, and does not run analytics scripts.

## What we store

If you change the language, the choice is saved in your browser under the key `winzu.lang` in `localStorage`. That value is a language code such as `en` or `es`. It never leaves your device through this site.

## What we do not collect

We do not operate user accounts. We do not collect names, business records, or uploaded files through this site. We do not run first-party analytics on this page.

## Third-party links

Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. Linked services may process data under their own policies when you use them. This notice covers only what this Winzu page itself stores.
