## I. Data Controller

**Who is responsible for your data?**

**Controller:** winzu.es

## II. Purpose of Processing and Categories of Data Processed

**What categories of data do we process and for what purpose?**

### User Account Management

- **Data:** First name, last name, email address, password (encrypted), date of birth (optional), postal address (optional), phone number (optional).
- **Purpose:** Create and manage user accounts, authentication, password recovery, and personalization of the experience.
- **Legal basis:** Consent of the data subject (Art. 6.1.a GDPR).

### Order Processing

- **Data:** First name, last name, shipping address, billing address, email address, phone number, payment information (without storing sensitive data such as full card numbers).
- **Purpose:** Manage orders, process payments, arrange shipments, handle returns, and provide customer support.
- **Legal basis:** Performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

### Marketing Communications (with consent)

- **Data:** Email address, marketing preferences (if provided).
- **Purpose:** Send commercial communications about winzu.es products and services.
- **Legal basis:** Consent of the data subject (Art. 6.1.a GDPR).

### Customer Support

- **Data:** First name, last name, email address, phone number, information about the inquiry.
- **Purpose:** Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
- **Legal basis:** Performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) or legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) for pre-contractual inquiries.

### Platform Improvement (anonymized data)

- **Data:** Anonymized browsing data (anonymized IP address, browser type, pages visited).
- **Purpose:** Analyze platform usage to improve its operation and the user experience.
- **Legal basis:** Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

### Video Surveillance (at physical stores)

- **Data:** Images captured by security cameras.
- **Purpose:** Ensure the security of people and premises.
- **Legal basis:** Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR), with due balancing of the rights of data subjects.
- **Retention period:** Maximum of one month, unless required for the investigation of a crime.

Data marked with an asterisk (\*) in forms are mandatory for the provision of the corresponding service. Refusal to provide them will make it impossible to provide that service.

## III. Lawfulness of Processing

The lawfulness of processing your data is based on:

- **Consent of the data subject:** For user registration, sending commercial communications, and other processing that requires explicit consent.
- **Performance of a contract:** For order management, service provision, and customer support related to a contract.
- **Legitimate interest:** For platform improvement (anonymized data) and video surveillance, always subject to an appropriate balancing of the rights of data subjects.
- **Compliance with a legal obligation:** Where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

## IV. Disclosure of Data

Your data may be disclosed to:

- **Service providers:** (e.g. web hosting, payment processing, email delivery) acting as processors and bound by contractual data-protection obligations.
- **Competent authorities:** In compliance with legal obligations.

## V. International Transfers

Where international data transfers are made, winzu.es will ensure that adequate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission or adequacy decisions.

## VI. User Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data.
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your data when they are no longer necessary.
- **Restrict** the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
- **Object** to the processing of your data.
- **Request portability** of your data.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
- Lodge a **complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency** (www.agpd.es).

To exercise your rights, you may contact us through [contact email address or form].

## VII. Other Data We Process on winzu.es

In addition to the data mentioned above, we may process other data in the context of our relationship with customers:

- **Telephone Support:** In order to improve the quality of our services and manage requests, we may record telephone calls. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of winzu.es in improving customer support.
- **Help Chat:** The help chat on our website is conducted anonymously. We record the chat history in anonymized form for analysis and service improvement.
- **Parking Control:** At some sites, we use cameras to control parking, recording vehicle license plates for a period of 24 hours to manage access and stay.

## VIII. Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Not applicable under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Spanish Organic Law on Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD).
